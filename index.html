Auto-Park

3 ideas

Tabs you haven’t touched park themselves.

Pick a limit, from an hour to a few weeks. When a tab sits unused that long, TabSidecar closes it and keeps it under AutoParked, where you can search it and reopen it in one click.

  • Pinned tabs never park.
  • Locked tabs never park, however long they sit.
  • Tabs playing sound never park.

Session restore

after every restartAnchored

Chrome restarts. Your windows come back.

A crash, a force-quit, an update, a laptop that ran out of battery. TabSidecar keeps a copy of every window as you work and matches each one back when Chrome opens again, with its name, color and locked tabs.

  • Named windows first. The windows you named, saved or anchored are listed on top, ready to restore.
  • The rest folds away. Untitled windows wait under Other windows. Hide one and it moves to Closed Windows.
  • Nothing is lost. The Last session page lists every window your last session had, and whether it came back.

Anchor the window you open every morning. It’s the only one that gets this teal, and it always comes back.

Saved Windows

open in one click

Save a window once. Open it any time.

Your launch-week tabs, your morning reading, the dozen docs a project needs. Save the window and close it. When you need it again, one click opens it with every tab in place.

Opening a saved window doesn’t use it up. It stays saved for next time.

Cloud Sync

optional, with an account

New laptop. Same windows.

Sign in and TabSidecar keeps a snapshot of your setup in the cloud, updated every hour. On a new computer or a fresh browser profile, sign in again and import it. Everything comes back the way you left it.

Each device keeps about a week of snapshots, so you can go back to an earlier one. Cloud Sync is optional and stays off unless you sign in.

Questions

6
Does Auto-Park delete my tabs?
No. A parked tab is closed, not forgotten. It’s saved under AutoParked with its title and address, and reopens in one click.
Which tabs never park?
Pinned tabs, locked tabs and tabs playing audio. Lock any tab from its right-click menu.
What happens if Chrome crashes?
TabSidecar saves your windows in the background. When Chrome starts again, it matches each window back and shows you what it restored.
Where are my tabs stored?
In your browser, on your computer. Cloud Sync is optional and stays off unless you sign in.
What does Cloud Sync leave out?
Your sign-in session and the history of single tabs you closed. Closed windows are included. Each device keeps about a week of snapshots, so you can go back to an earlier one.
Does it cost anything?
No. TabSidecar is free. You only need an account if you want Cloud Sync.
